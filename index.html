Progressive exhale holds

Build this standard exercise automatically. Each round is exhale → hold → normal breathing. Normal-breathing periods decrease each round, but never go below 5 seconds. The final round ends after its hold.

Normal breathing: 45, 30, 15, 5 seconds · 5 holds total

Breath-hold safety

Practise seated or lying down in a safe place. Never practise breath holds in water, while driving, standing, or operating machinery. Never force an exhale or hold. Breathe normally whenever needed, and stop immediately if you feel dizzy, faint, short of breath, or unwell. If you have a medical condition or are unsure whether breath-holding is suitable for you, check with a healthcare professional first.
